NOTE:

The particle is a 1 micrometer radius polystyrene sphere immersed in water at a temperature of 293K.
The viscosity of water at this temperature is 0.001 Pa s.

The format of the files D0b2_*.lvm.txt is the following:


First  column:  time (in seconds) 
Second column: Voltage signal (x)
Third  column: Voltage signal (y)
Fourth column: Voltage signal (z)
